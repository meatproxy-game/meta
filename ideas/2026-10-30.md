# an idea

> A survival horror game, but it's a boring office job
> - luke (discussing his own idea that focused more on the dystopian aspects of modern office life)

then later...

> a tech worker watches the people around him become meatproxies
> - goos (this)

Target maybe 6~8hr gameplay (ambitious i know)

three? four? story arches?

not autobiographical. completely fiction. set straddling the past / present / future.

## "smell the grind"

- after escaping retail hell, you build a sense of security in your new IT helpdesk job.
- your eyes close.
- your career soars.

## "a golden age"

- your bluepilled era.
- the most productive you've ever been.
- the most productive you'll ever be.
- all around you, the world is laying the groundwork for the shitshow that is coming.
- your heart slowly sinks.
- your eyes slowly open.
- your career plateaus.
- your passion burns out.

## "office complex"

- midlevel work burns you out.
- a new job offer comes up. lots of money being thrown on the table.
- the catch? 6mo forced RTO for "onboarding" and "teambuilding".
- a calculated payment.
- soul for money, in a time when money still promises it can buy the sense of security they've stolen from you.
- deep in the belly of the machine.
- your eyes are wide open now.
- people start to offload all their thinking to machines.
- the people who own those machines use this to own them inside and out.
- people and robots become indistinguishable.
- you start to crave the revolution.

## "afterwards (happy)"

- the revolution starts.
- you escape to a cabin in the woods.
- you make local friends.
- you help them build.
- they help you build.
- the world looks a little brighter.

## "afterwards (sad)"

- you start to submit
- you become a meatproxy yourself.
- eventually you become part of the machine yourself.
